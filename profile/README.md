# T&I

### Technology, Innovation & Practical Software Solutions

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **T&I**, a Japan-based technology company focused on software development, IT solutions, AI, automation, and cross-border technology collaboration.

🌐 **Website:** https://www.t-and-i.biz/

---

## 🚀 About T&I

At T&I, we use technology to solve real-world problems and improve the way people and businesses work.

Our work covers software and system development, web technologies, automation, AI-powered solutions, and IT consulting.

We also value collaboration across borders and actively explore opportunities that connect technology, businesses, and people between **Japan and China**.

---

## 💡 What We Do

* 💻 **Software & System Development**
  Development of practical software and business systems.

* 🌐 **Web Development**
  Web applications, websites, APIs, and related technologies.

* 🤖 **AI & Automation**
  Exploring AI-powered applications, workflow automation, LLMs, and tools that improve productivity.

* 🛠️ **Developer Tools & Utilities**
  Building useful tools for developers, creators, and everyday users.

* ☁️ **IT Infrastructure & Integration**
  Exploring practical solutions involving cloud services, self-hosted systems, APIs, and system integration.

* 🌏 **Offshore Development & IT Consulting**
  Supporting technology collaboration and development projects across Japan and China.
